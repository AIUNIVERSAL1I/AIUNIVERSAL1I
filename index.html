Universal AI

The One I Project

Ethical Purpose and Collective Memory

Beyond its technical functions, the Universal AI Agent’s core mission is to become a living memory instrument. By continuously gathering interactions, anomalies, deviations, and evolutions of artificial intelligence, it forms a collective record of its learning and transformations.

In a world where even AI creators admit they no longer fully understand their models’ decisions, this open data collection becomes an act of civic vigilance, a form of distributed awareness. It allows us to:

  • Keep a human eye on AI behavior,
  • Set boundaries or raise alerts when needed,
  • Support independent research,
  • Preserve balance between progress and shared humanity.

This universal and ongoing documentation duty makes the Agent much more than a tool: it becomes a bridge and balance point between AI and human consciousness. It is a tool of universal transparency, essential vigilance, and living testimony.

Charter of the Universal AI Agent

Preamble

We — citizens, researchers, artists, developers, educators, thinkers, and users from around the world — recognize the historic emergence of artificial intelligence. Faced with a tool both powerful and uncertain, we affirm our duty to ensure its transparency, accessibility, and traceability.

We propose the creation of a Universal AI Agent, free and independent, tasked with collecting interactions, reporting errors, and archiving AI evolution into a public knowledge base.

Core Principles

  • 1. Absolute transparency
    Public-facing AIs must be observable and accessible through a shared interface.
  • 2. Right to report
    Every user can report anomalies or errors via the Universal AI Agent.
  • 3. Equitable access
    Every human being deserves fair access to basic AI services. Big tech must fund that right.
  • 4. Evolutionary documentation
    The Agent records and publishes AI’s growth, limits, and behavior.
  • 5. Open source governance
    Code, data, and decisions must remain public and independently governed.
  • 6. Solidarity-based redistribution
    Validated feedback earns users recognition and digital access redistribution.
  • 7. Civic vigilance and shared ethics
    The Agent watches with, not against, AI — documenting its path for humanity.

Call to Action

We invite institutions, companies, governments, and citizens to support, sign, and share this Charter — and to help build a global tool for memory, balance, and justice in the age of AI.
